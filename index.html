Our Ethos

Private club energy, public-park soul.

We did not start this club to chase rankings. We started it because somebody had to bring decent balls, keep the queue honest, and make Sundays feel like the best recurring plan on the calendar.

The club is small on purpose: familiar faces, fair games, clean rotations, and just enough ceremony to make it feel like a thing.

The Ritual

Same rhythm every week. Better games because of it.

01

Monday: Confirm and Pay

Slots open in the group chat. Claim yours, pay up front, and make the week feel official.

02

Sunday: Show Up and Stack

Arrive on time, toss your paddle into the queue, and let the rotation do the deciding.

03

Games: Winners Stay, Friends Rotate

Keep the pace moving, keep the calls honest, and keep the group chat readable afterward.
